
# 🌟 FinTrack Pro – Personal Finance Tracker

**FinTrack Pro** is a modern, user-friendly web application designed to help you take full control of your finances. Track your income, expenses, budgets, and savings—all in one place, with sleek visuals, intuitive features, and support for multiple currencies.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **📊 Dashboard**  
  Get a quick overview of your total balance, income, expenses, and savings rate—all in one clean interface.

- **💸 Transactions Management**  
  Easily add, edit, delete, and filter your income and expense entries.

- **📅 Budget Planning**  
  Set monthly or yearly budgets for various categories and monitor your spending habits.

- **📈 Reports & Insights**  
  Generate insightful visual reports for income, expenses, categories, and savings trends.

- **🌍 Multi-Currency Support**  
  Choose from over 20 world currencies and customize how they're displayed.

- **🎨 Themes & Personalization**  
  Switch between Light, Dark, Sunset, and Mint themes to match your style.

- **🔐 Data Privacy First**  
  Your data is 100% private—stored locally in your browser and never sent to any server.
